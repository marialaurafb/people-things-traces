As a collaborator on Mapping TIFF, a digital project created by Dr. Constanza Burucúa that explores the history of the Toronto International Film Festival (TIFF), I updated the website with festival data for three additional years, extending the resource’s historical coverage. My contribution involved preparing and integrating new data into the existing digital platform, supporting the ongoing documentation and public accessibility of the festival’s history. The project demonstrates how structured data and data visualization can support the exploration and interpretation of cultural events.

[Click here](https://mappingtiff-westernu.hub.arcgis.com/) to access the website.
