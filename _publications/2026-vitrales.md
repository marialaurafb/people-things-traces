This chapter argues that studying stained glass in Guadalajara through a combination of mapping, case study analysis, and participatory cataloguing offers a valuable approach to rethinking the history of modern religious architecture in the city and bringing visibility to an artistic field that has remained largely on the margins of art historiography.
