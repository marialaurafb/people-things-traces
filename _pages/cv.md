Work experience
======

  * Faculty of Information and Media Studies, Western University, London, ON
  * Duties includes: Conducting my research project "Rebuilding Memory, Shaping Futures" that includes activating a photo archive with the community, the virtual reconstruction of destroyed villages, co-authoring a book about the history of Copapayo with a community historian, and updating and managing the massacre map on ArcGIS
  * Supervisor: Amanda Grzyb

* Fall 2022: Lecturer
  * Department of Visual Arts, Faculty of Arts and Humanities, Western University, London, ON
  * Course: Baroque in Europe and the Iberian Territories
  * Duties: Lectures, lesson planning, rubric, activities, and course design, marking and grading, monitoring an online forum

* Fall 2017 - Winter 2022: Instructor
  * Department of Languages, Cultures, and Linguistics, Faculty of Arts and Humanities, Western University, London, ON
  * Courses: Spanish for Beginners and Intermediate Spanish
  * Duties: Lectures, marking and grading, designing teaching materials, creating oral exam rubrics

* Summer 2021 - Winter 2025: Graduate Research Assistant
  * Surviving Memory in Postwar El Salvador, Faculty of Information and Media Studies, Western University, London, ON
  * Duties includes: Transcription and translation of testimones, coordination of the video editing team, facilitating workshops and other fieldwork in El Salvador, working on the backend of the massacre map on ArcGIS
  * Supervisor: Amanda Grzyb
